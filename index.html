Data sources and limitations

NASA POWER: daily maximum air temperature, corrected precipitation, and modeled surface soil wetness. Source-native grid resolution varies by parameter and is much coarser than a field.

Open-Meteo: seven-day forecast from an automatically selected weather model; resolution varies by region. This is a forecast, not an observation.
