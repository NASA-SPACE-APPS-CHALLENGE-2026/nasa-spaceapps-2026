PlotTwist logo ideas: twists and plants

Round 2. Every idea combines a growing plant with a twist or spiral.

1 · Tendril sprout
A seedling with one leaf and a curling tendril at the top, like a pea plant.
2 · Braided stems
Two crops twisted around each other like a braid, a picture of rotation. Each ends in its own leaf.
3 · Twisted leaf
A long leaf with a half twist in the middle, so its dark and light sides swap.
4 · Question-mark sprout
The stem curls into a question mark with a leaf at the tip. A plot twist is a surprise.
5 · Fiddlehead
A young fern frond coiled into a spiral, about to unroll. Suggests new growth.
6 · Rows into stem
Two field rows sweep in from each side, twist around each other, and grow up into one sprout.
7 · Leaf pinwheel
Curved leaves arranged so they look like they're spinning, like crops cycling through the seasons.
8 · Spiral seed
A seed with a spiral on it, sprouting. The twist is where growth starts.
