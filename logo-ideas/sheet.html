PlotTwist logo ideas

Brand colours: field green #2f6b3a, sky blue #2b7fd0, wheat gold #c9971f

1 · Twisted rows
Crop rows that bend and change colour partway through, for a field changing course.
2 · The odd plot
A grid of field plots with one tilted out of line. The "twist" in PlotTwist.
3 · Orbiting sprout
A satellite orbiting a seedling, for NASA data looking after the field.
4 · Rotation cycle
Three arrows, one per crop, looping around the soil. Shows crop rotation directly.
5 · Field pin
A map pin with a sprout and rows inside: this field, at this location.
6 · Soil profile
A cut-away of soil layers with a twisting root. Fits the new ecology focus.
7 · PT monogram
Bold P and T, with a leaf growing off the T. Works well as a small app icon.
8 · Planet field
Curved field rows on the edge of the Earth, as seen from orbit.
